given a 0-indexed array nums & positive integer k:
 
Example : nums: [2, 6, 5, 8], k = 2

You can do following operation on the array any number of times
---
Choose 2 distinct indicies : i and j and simultaneously update the values of 

nums[i] = nums[i] AND nums[j]
nums[j] = nums[i] OR nums[j]

"AND", "OR" => bitwise operations 

---

have to choose k elements from the final array and calculate the sum of their squares.

return maximum sum of squares

---

Constraints:

1 <= k <= nums.length <= 10^5
1 <= nums[i] <= 10^9

---
